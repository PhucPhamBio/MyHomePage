## Function lives in context

Proteins assigned to the same broad functional class can recognize different substrates or catalyze different reactions. Sequence similarity is valuable, but it can blur the local geometry that makes a transformation possible.

Structure-aware models can represent residue neighborhoods, pockets, and spatial motifs. Their value is not simply higher accuracy. A strong model should help connect a prediction to an interpretable catalytic arrangement that a scientist can inspect.
