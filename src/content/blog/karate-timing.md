## Fast is not early

Beginners often try to move faster. Experienced practice reveals a different problem: noticing the moment when movement becomes useful.

Timing depends on distance, posture, breathing, and attention. The lesson travels beyond the dojo. In research, a precise question asked at the right moment can outperform a large amount of hurried work.
