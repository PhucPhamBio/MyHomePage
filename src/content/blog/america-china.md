## Look beyond the scoreboard

Comparisons between the United States and China often begin with publication counts, funding, or patents. Those indicators matter, but they do not fully describe how researchers are trained, how teams form, or how ideas move between institutions.

The more useful stories are often local: a lab meeting, a shared dataset, a student choosing a field, or a collaboration that survives political tension. Good writing should preserve those human details while still examining the systems around them.
